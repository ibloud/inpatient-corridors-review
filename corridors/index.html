Inpatient Corridors · Experimental cooperative variant

Build a way out.
Leave together.

Two people, one shared device—or explore both turns yourself. Gather Support, build Care, and reach the exits before Pressure reaches 10.

How to play · start here
  1. Player 1 starts. Each player takes two actions, then passes the device.
  2. Tap an adjacent open square to move. Gather adds Support; each gather adds 1 Tension until Creative Space is built.
  3. Contribute 1 Support at a time to Care projects. Both players fund the same projects.
  4. After both turns, Pressure rises by 1 and barriers form. Spend Support to ease Pressure or clear adjacent barriers.
  5. Win together: complete four Care projects, including Housing and either Peer Support or Mobile Response; keep Pressure at 3 or less; get both pawns to either exit.

Rest reduces your pawn's Tension by 2. At the start of a turn with Tension 6, it resets to 4 and adds 1 Pressure. These are game counters, not measurements of either player.
