# 50 Ways to Leave Another — Case Study

This directory is the public-facing case-study layer.

It does **not** replace or rewrite the underlying project. It gives a reviewer a transparent way to distinguish evidence from inference.

## Evidence model

### 1. Public record

Publicly observable Ren site surfaces and published Creative Deploy material.

Creative Deploy publicly describes Sick Boi as a **Headless Shopify + Sanity** implementation and separately lists **Ren — Marketing Website — Interactive Experience** on its work portfolio. The Ren listing links to the Sick Boi case-study page; no separate Creative Deploy write-up of the Ren marketing site is published there.

This does **not** establish Ren's private brief, exact production architecture, private analytics, or any undocumented requirement.

### 2. Our build

Yellow Door and Rabbit Hole are independently inspectable demonstrations of browser narrative/runtime work, structured content, reproducible builds, accessibility-oriented interaction, and portable artifacts.

They are evidence of capability. They are not represented as missing pieces of Ren's production site.

### 3. Unknown

Private source repositories, credentials, private analytics, original briefs, internal architecture decisions, and other material not directly available remain unknown.

## Review standard

The case study should never imply access to a production system, private client brief, private financial records, or legal status that has not been verified.

The intended question is:

> What does the evidence support, what did we actually build, and what remains unknown?

## Public benchmark

The Creative Deploy Sick Boi case study is a documented benchmark for that project's headless Shopify + Sanity architecture. It is **not** evidence that Ren's private production architecture is identical.

The public Ren pages establish visible interactive/music surfaces. They establish what a visitor can observe, not the private implementation behind those surfaces.

## Graveyard standard

The graveyard records discarded approaches and decisions, not successful proof modules merely because they are part of the project's history.

Current recorded discarded approaches include:

- **55-day four-zone community sprint:** reduced to a Zone 1 vertical slice rather than attempting the full four-zone program at once.
- **UEFN as a web-developer recruitment path:** dropped because the relevant skills did not transfer cleanly to the browser/web development need.
- **“What the Ren site was intended to be”:** dropped as a claim when the Creative Deploy case study was established as a Sick Boi benchmark rather than evidence of Ren's private brief or architecture.
- **Competitive parity table:** replaced by the three-layer evidence model because apparent feature parity is not proof of private production architecture.
- **Ren reconstruction:** stopped at the case-study/reference boundary. No claim of production ownership or access.

Yellow Door and Rabbit Hole remain in the repository as **our-build capability evidence**, not as graveyard failures.

## Made Sick

**Made Sick** is our consent-first creator-directory/project organization. It is run independently of Ren and has **no affiliation with Ren Makes Music or Sick Boi**. The name is used here to describe the project's proposed destination for any remainder after documented expenses; it is not presented as a Ren organization, partner, or beneficiary unless a separate written agreement establishes that relationship.
