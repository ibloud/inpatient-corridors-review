What the event is

The proposal uses fictional clues and fabricated documents, public-space investigation, optional participation, and an accessible indoor fallback. It is an event-format companion study, not a replacement for the Yellow Door interactive.

Participant access

Play the game → win / complete the game → receive the Discord invitation → meet and coordinate with invited participants.

Discord is not the public entry point. The invitation is the transition into the participant coordination space. The Violet's Revenge community guide identifies Discord as its day-to-day communication and playtesting-coordination space while GitHub remains the project source of truth.

Public safety resource

HeartSupport is provided as a public support resource outside participant coordination. It is not a substitute for emergency services, clinical care, or event staff response.

Planned structure

  • Riverfront / Stone Arch and Mill Ruins area
  • Breakfast briefing
  • Whittier pop-up case-file phase
  • Public landmark clue trail
  • Accessible indoor reset / meal period
  • Final clue and check-in at The Cabooze

Safety and participation

  • Participation remains optional at every stop.
  • Buddy/check-in practices and a clear emergency contact.
  • No recording by default; visible photo zones only.
  • No stored location tracking.
  • No requirement to disclose personal information to participate.
  • Indoor/tabletop fallback for unsafe weather or walking conditions.

Rights and provenance

This is an independent design/event study. The existence of a proposal does not establish artist participation, approval, endorsement, partnership, venue confirmation, ticketing, or rights clearance. Public release remains subject to the project's existing rights and safety rules.
