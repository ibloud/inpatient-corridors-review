What the event is

The proposal uses fictional clues and fabricated documents, public-space investigation, optional participation, and an accessible indoor fallback. It is an event-format companion study, not a replacement for the Yellow Door interactive.

Participant access

Play the game → win / complete the game → receive the Discord invitation → meet and coordinate with invited participants.

Discord is not the public entry point. The invitation is the transition into the participant coordination space. The Violet's Revenge community guide identifies Discord as its day-to-day communication and playtesting-coordination space while GitHub remains the project source of truth.

Public safety resource

HeartSupport is provided as a public support resource outside participant coordination. It is not a substitute for emergency services, clinical care, or event staff response.

Planned structure

  • Riverfront / Stone Arch and Mill Ruins area
  • Breakfast briefing
  • Whittier pop-up case-file phase
  • Public landmark clue trail
  • Accessible indoor reset / meal period
  • Choose an ending: a sober, family-friendly trail or the separate adult concert option at The Cabooze

Sober, family-friendly adventure

Families and people who prefer an alcohol-free outing can complete the Forensics Trail without entering a bar or attending a concert. This is a proposed self-guided branch, not a hosted tour or an announcement of venue partnerships. The shared fictional clue and reflection activity can finish before the evening: “What did someone design for another person to use?” No purchase, score, health disclosure, game victory, or Discord membership is required to follow this public route. Adults remain responsible for the children in their care.

  • Minneapolis Institute of Art (Mia) in Whittier: observe an artwork or designed object and sketch an optional clue. General admission is free; published Saturday hours are 10 a.m.–5 p.m. Verify October 31 access before setting a meeting time.
  • Mill City Museum and the riverfront: look for a machine, building, or city system and compare it to the fictional case file. Museum admission, hours, accessibility and travel between stops need a final check. The public riverfront can serve as a shorter outdoor alternative.
  • Finish with a meal or a family Halloween activity. Barebones Halloween Extravaganza lists an October 31 outdoor puppet pageant at Powderhorn Park, 7 p.m. (gates 6 p.m.), with pay-what-you-can admission. It is an independent, optional evening destination, with separate travel, weather and ticket decisions.

The Bakken Museum is an optional science and invention detour rather than a required stop. Its October 31 Inventors Club is a separately registered, multiweek youth program; it is not a drop-in event activity. Any market or trick-or-treat listing should be added only after its 2026 date and hours are confirmed.

Two endings: The family branch ends with its own clue and optional reflection. The Cabooze concert is a separate adult option and is never a requirement for completion. Confirm its age policy and all venue details before publishing an operational itinerary. No named venue has agreed to host this project.

Safety and participation

  • Participation remains optional at every stop.
  • Buddy/check-in practices and a clear emergency contact.
  • No recording by default; visible photo zones only.
  • No stored location tracking.
  • No requirement to disclose personal information to participate.
  • Indoor/tabletop fallback for unsafe weather or walking conditions.
