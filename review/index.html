The request is not "approve this." We are looking for observations, questions, boundaries, contradictions, and things that should be tested next.

What your review can influence

  • Gameplay / system: next paper-playtest iteration, rule clarification, balance tests, or deferred mechanics.
  • Participant / host: front-door language, facilitator materials, and replication documentation.
  • Safety / community care: boundaries, escalation procedures, resource language, and external qualified review.
  • Accessibility: interface, format, pacing, alternative participation modes, and documentation.
  • Rights / provenance: permissions, attribution, governance, correction, withdrawal, and adaptation rules.
  • Replication / facilitation: host packet, staffing assumptions, materials, localization, and operational questions.
  • Claims / evidence: wording of claims, hypotheses, success measures, and what evidence must exist before release.

Feedback does not itself establish endorsement, affiliation, authorship, clinical validation, or permission to use another person's work.

What this project is not

This is an arts-and-engagement prototype. It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, crisis care, or clinical research. Participation is voluntary; reviewers are not being asked to disclose personal health information.

Event-specific operational details remain proposals until independently verified. Review of the event concept is different from operational approval of an event.
