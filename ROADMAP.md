# Roadmap and Stop Gates

## Stage 0 — Private review

- Deliver this proposal without claiming affiliation.
- Receive a clear decline, review request, or invitation to discuss.
- **Stop gate:** no response is not consent.

## Stage 1 — Rights and partner design

- Confirm participants and authorized representatives.
- Agree on scope, ownership, compensation, approvals, credits, withdrawal, and publicity.
- Recruit lived-experience, accessibility, community-care, and facilitation partners.
- **Stop gate:** do not use protected creative material before authorization.

## Stage 2 — Original prototype

- Replace person-based archetypes with player-selected system states.
- Build with original code, text, sound, and art unless licensed assets are documented.
- Conduct accessibility and safety review.
- **Checkpoint:** closed internal playtest and written issue log.

## Stage 3 — Pilot

- Run three small facilitated sessions.
- Collect minimal anonymous program feedback.
- Publish changes, limitations, and partner contributions.
- **Checkpoint:** partners approve the public impact summary.

## Stage 4 — Public release

- Release the accessible prototype, facilitator guide, resources, and provenance ledger.
- Provide a contact for corrections and removal requests.
- **Checkpoint:** verify every public attribution and link.

## Stage 5 — Recognition decision

- Compare completed evidence with live award rules.
- Obtain written approval from every named entrant or credited principal.
- Submit only accurate, substantiated claims.
