Artist starter: lyrics, images, story and release handoff

This is a development starting point, not a final release recipe. Prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance, 19 October 2026.

The artist leads; AI supports the work

ChatGPT does not replace the artist. It can carry analytical, organizational and technical tasks the artist does not want to manage alone: source records, metadata, budgets, choice maps, file preparation and checks. The artist supplies meaning, voice and approval. “Left-brained work” is shorthand for these tasks, not a neurological claim.

Yellow Door is not a faithful adaptation of the EP. Its revised image framing does not make it one. AI can draft original scenes around broad themes, but cannot infer artistic intent or claim approval from third-party lyric listings. Start with the artist’s approved material, review one scene, and keep every generated addition editable. Generated source is not a final release.

Lyrics are release infrastructure

An artist-approved lyric record prevents creative work from depending on incomplete search results or crowd transcriptions. Keep one versioned, exportable source of truth under artist/team control, with access appropriate to the work’s release status. A private working database, structured folder or existing catalogue system can serve this role; no new service or public upload is required. A public repository is not the default home for unreleased or third-party lyrics.

For each recording, record title, version (studio/live/clean/explicit), language, ISRC where assigned, linked composition identifier/ISWC where available, writers and publishers, approved lyric text, approver/date, revision history, timing-file version, permitted uses and correction contact. Keep composition and recording identifiers distinct. Link the timed lyrics to the exact audio master; a new edit or performance may require new timings.

From that source, prepare destination-specific exports and retain submission receipts, approval status and corrections. Merely storing lyrics in a database or on Genius does not automatically propagate them to Apple, Shopify, EVEN or every other service. Use each provider’s supported authorized delivery route.

Discovery, singing and covers

Correct lyrics support discovery by remembered words, comprehension, sing-along participation and reliable interpretation. They also give another artist a trustworthy textual starting point for learning a cover, with clear attribution and contact information. They do not supply a musical arrangement, pronunciation coaching or performance license by themselves.

Apple Music for Artists currently supports adding and correcting static lyrics in Artist Content. Provider delivery supports time-synced lyrics in TTML; Apple specifies that timing must match the exact audio version. Apple Music Sing’s adjustable vocals and beat-by-beat display have their own delivery/eligibility requirements: uploading plain text does not guarantee Sing availability. Musixmatch provides another verified-artist lyric distribution route; confirm the appropriate destination rather than assuming every route updates every platform.

Published lyrics do not grant another artist permission to reproduce lyric text, release a cover recording, use the original master, synchronize music to video, translate/adapt lyrics or manufacture a karaoke product. Those are different uses whose permissions and applicable licensing requirements must be established. Record the intended use and refer it to the relevant rights holder, publisher, distributor or qualified adviser. Do not advertise an open cover/karaoke license merely because words are visible online.

Ask ChatGPT for a starting point

Copy this prompt and fill in only the material you choose to share:

I am an independent artist. Help me develop a short original interactive pilot, not a final release. I have never used Ink or EVEN. Work in [short paragraphs / dictation-friendly questions / my preferred format], one small step at a time. My functional access needs are [optional; no diagnosis needed].

First ask up to three questions about my intended feeling, audience and the original material I already have, then wait. Keep a resumable checkpoint. I may skip a question.

Use only my approved lyrics, a permitted summary or clearly identified sources. If you cannot access the lyrics, say so; do not infer the song’s meaning from its title. Separate visible photo descriptions, source evidence, my interpretation and your invented suggestions. Do not diagnose people or invent their motives. Keep credits separate from alt text.

Propose a three-to-five-minute outline with two or three meaningful choices and a complete ending. Provide a small table: source/theme, photograph, scene/choice, strength of connection and unresolved question. Where the connection is weak, say so rather than forcing it. Do not copy the song’s plot, lyrics or another artist’s identity into new fiction without permission.

Show one sample scene and ask for my approval before expanding. Once approved, give me a linear version, choice map, source/credit record and the next small revision. Preserve what I authored and mark AI additions. Do not call it finished, cleared, tested or artist-approved merely because you generated it.

Only after the story works, explain Ink in plain language and offer an optional source draft if I request it. Label generated Ink uncompiled until compiled and tested. Keep story completion independent of audio, purchases, signups or AI use.

Suggest one optional handoff to an existing official album/product page. Distinguish an external Shopify link from an API/cart integration. Do not invent a product, stock limit, price, earnings result, partner or artist attachment. Never publish, change my account, contact anyone or submit a release without my authorization.

What the Yellow Door demonstrates

The current visual-framing revision improves image descriptions and connects editorial prompts to the existing corridor decisions. It does not rewrite the Ink narrative into the EP’s story. The song-to-story comparison finds weak and partial connections; see the mapping assessment. A better starting point for a new artist is to build from their own intended meaning and approved material, then let them reject the proposed connections.

Existing official album and storefront links provide an optional exit after the complete story. That is a demonstrable handoff, not platform account access, a connected cart or a cleared commercial bundle.
