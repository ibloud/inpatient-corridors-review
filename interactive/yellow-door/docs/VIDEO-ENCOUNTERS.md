# Yellow Door visual encounters and optional music page

The six entry encounters now show project-provided still images instead of relying on six third-party video embeds. Each screen offers a direct YouTube link and album links for Apple Music and Spotify. Visitors can skip the visual sequence and enter the Ink story immediately.

After the Ink story ends, an optional button opens a separate listening screen. That screen embeds the supplied “So The Story Goes...” YouTube video and offers direct links if the embed is blocked by the browser or video publisher. The listening screen does not prevent the story from ending.

The encounter titles follow the supplied *Sick Sick Soul (Vol. 1)* sequence. Dominique identifies the uploaded stills as coming from Ren's Instagram post “Oh we do like to be beside the sea side,” with credits on the post to @jakewiiliams, @spaaaacey, and @chloeimbach. The project owner limits this use to credited, noncommercial demonstration under a fair-use claim. This is a post-level credit, not a verified mapping of each photo to a photographer. The claim has not been independently assessed and does not extend to sale of merchandise. The assignment of stills to screens is provisional pending visual review.

Album destinations were resolved in the Apple Music and Spotify catalogs. YouTube video IDs and playlist URL were supplied in this project; successful playback of individual embeds depends on YouTube and browser restrictions.
