# Yellow Door — Six Video Encounters

The Yellow Door now treats the six public music videos associated with *Sick Sick Soul (Vol. 1)* as **actual encounters inside the interactive**, rather than merely presenting the playlist as a link.

## Sequence

1. **So The Story Goes...**
2. **CTRL ALT DELETE**
3. **Truth or Dare**
4. **Dream Life**
5. **Two's On A Cigarette**
6. **Pink Heineken**

The sequence is presented through the supplied public YouTube playlist using YouTube's playlist embed. Each encounter gets its own Yellow Door screen, progress marker, framing text, and continuation choice. After the sixth encounter, the existing Ink corridor opens.

## Narrative structure

**Yellow Door → video encounter 1 → video encounter 2 → video encounter 3 → video encounter 4 → video encounter 5 → video encounter 6 → corridor**

The playlist remains the external source. The Yellow Door does not download, rehost, alter, or claim ownership of the videos.

Inside the experience, however, the visitor encounters the six works one at a time as part of the threshold into the corridor. The music is therefore a story mechanism rather than a promotional sidebar.
