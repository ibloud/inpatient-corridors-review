# Yellow Door — Six Video Encounters

The Yellow Door treats the six public music videos associated with *Sick Sick Soul (Vol. 1)* as **actual encounters inside the interactive**, rather than merely presenting the playlist as a link.

## Sequence

1. **So The Story Goes...** — `u1qtyMPokZM`
2. **CTRL ALT DELETE** — `X--PXyB1Zw0`
3. **Truth or Dare** — `FmaBhsRfhIw`
4. **Dream Life** — `0HhRNbZ0wRY`
5. **Two's On A Cigarette** — `1WCfWxgEY8E`
6. **Pink Heineken** — `Ra8gSw7Djvo`

Yellow Door presents these as **six individually addressed YouTube video encounters, in the supplied SICK SICK SOUL sequence**. The playlist is retained as source/context, but the interactive does **not** depend on playlist position.

Each encounter gets its own Yellow Door screen, progress marker, framing text, and continuation choice. After the sixth encounter, the existing Ink corridor opens.

## Narrative structure

**Yellow Door → video encounter 1 → video encounter 2 → video encounter 3 → video encounter 4 → video encounter 5 → video encounter 6 → corridor**

The videos are embedded from YouTube using their exact supplied video IDs. The Yellow Door does not download, rehost, alter, or claim ownership of the videos.

Inside the experience, the visitor encounters the six works one at a time as part of the threshold into the corridor. The music is therefore a story mechanism rather than a promotional sidebar.

## Attribution boundary

The works are presented as public external media by **Ren & The Skinner Brothers**. The implementation does not imply that the artists selected, commissioned, approved, endorsed, or participated in the Yellow Door.

The project controls the framing and sequencing of the encounter only.

No additional personal, medical, biographical, ideological, or family claims about the artists are made here.
