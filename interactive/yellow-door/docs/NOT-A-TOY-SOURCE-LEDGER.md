# NOT A TOY — public-source canon ledger

**Purpose:** collect evidence before authoring the Ink adventure. This is a research ledger, not a lore bible. It deliberately separates release metadata and interface motifs from narrative canon.

**Rule:** No story fact without a source. A source may confirm release metadata or a title; it does not automatically confirm plot, character, symbolism, artist intent, or ARG connections. Record the exact evidence and locator before making a narrative claim. When sources are insufficient, mark the question unresolved and request an approved reference.

## Supported adaptation vocabulary (not lore)

The release titles, loading interludes, archive/file naming, and game-related words may inform the adventure's UI and interaction mechanics: loading state, files, menus, navigation, progress, choices, branching, and system-style presentation. These are design choices by the adaptation, not statements about NOT A TOY's canon.

## Unresolved canon questions — do not fill by invention

- What exactly is “the maze,” and what is the intended meaning of “get out”? Capture the official post and context.
- Is there a named protagonist, player identity, or character roster? No source captured.
- Are any music videos explicitly connected as a narrative? No official explanation captured.
- Are there official hidden/ARG clues, sequence rules, or story endings? No official confirmation captured.
- What is the approved relationship between album sequence and any narrative? Track order alone is not proof.
- What is the comic's content and how, if at all, should it appear in the story? Read the product description/preview or request an approved reference.
