# Yellow Door — Independent Musical Breadcrumb

## Purpose

The Yellow Door includes an explicitly **independent musical breadcrumb**: a public YouTube playlist featuring *Sick Sick Soul (Vol. 1)* by Ren & The Skinner Brothers.

The intended relationship is:

**Yellow Door → listening breadcrumb → Ren & The Skinner Brothers**

The playlist is a listening path, not a claim about authorship of the Yellow Door, and not a statement that the artists selected, commissioned, approved, endorsed, or participated in this experience.

## Playlist

**SICK SICK SOUL — Ren & The Skinner Brothers**

Tracks presented in the playlist:

1. So The Story Goes...
2. CTRL ALT DELETE
3. Truth or Dare
4. Dream Life
5. Two's On A Cigarette
6. Pink Heineken

[Listen to the playlist on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLHbj3Gti2ieMLX14MIy5xvV0GPYMciUvz)

## Design posture

The breadcrumb is intentionally **followable rather than promotional**:

- It gives the visitor a musical thread to investigate.
- It does not turn the Yellow Door into a Ren biography or fan page.
- It does not imply a relationship between this project and Ren, The Skinner Brothers, their representatives, labels, vendors, or collaborators.
- It does not require a purchase, stream, signup, or other commercial action.
- The external playlist remains the source destination; the Yellow Door only points to it.

The public experience should preserve this distinction even if the breadcrumb becomes part of a larger convergence path.

## Attribution boundary

The playlist establishes a musical connection through the named collaborative release. It does **not**, by itself, establish any additional personal, medical, biographical, ideological, or creative claims about the artists.

Accordingly, no such claims are made here.
