The midpoint question

How should decisions be made when they affect people who were not included in making them?

The restriction does not need to be secretly evil. The people making the decision do not need to be villains. The tension comes from a familiar institutional gap: a decision can be reasonable in isolation and still create harm when people are excluded from the process.

What the next release should leave behind

  • Shared questions instead of a single “correct” interpretation.
  • More than one legitimate way to participate.
  • A visible shift from individual reaction to collective capacity.
  • A retrospective layer that documents what changed.
  • A structure that can support the next iteration without pretending the previous one was perfect.
