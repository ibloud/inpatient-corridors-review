Recognition Strategy

Primary fit: Anthem Awards — Health

The Anthem Awards currently evaluates impact work across criteria including concept, execution, impact and engagement, inclusivity, and capacity building. Those criteria align with a completed, measured community-engagement project.

Important: The current Season 6 deadline is September 18, 2026. A deadline is not a reason to submit an unfinished concept. Unless a qualifying pilot and permissions already exist, the responsible choice is to prepare evidence for a later cycle.

Potential entry type and exact category must be verified against the live entry system when the work is complete.

Secondary fit: The Webby Awards

A future finished experience may fit Games, Websites & Mobile Sites, Apps/Software/Immersive, or another current category. Eligibility windows and category definitions change; verify them at submission time.

Separate music path

Any GRAMMY submission for Asylum belongs to the recording's eligible submitting parties and the Recording Academy process. This repo neither creates recording eligibility nor controls artist submissions. It should not imply that an impact prototype can obtain a GRAMMY for Chris.

Credit rule

Award credit follows contracted, verifiable contribution. Before any entry:

  • Each credited party approves the entry text.
  • Ownership and entrant identity are documented.
  • Artist, producer, adviser, developer, facilitator, and community-partner roles are described accurately.
  • Outcome claims are supported by the published evaluation.
  • Entry fees and publicity permissions are approved.

Strategic goal

The relevant achievement is not “an award for Chris” arranged from outside. It is a body of work Chris can knowingly help shape and, if he chooses, stand behind—one strong enough for the appropriate institution to recognize his actual contribution.
