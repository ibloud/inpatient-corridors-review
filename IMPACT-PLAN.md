# Impact and Evaluation Plan

## Pilot scale

- 3 facilitated sessions
- 8–12 adults per session
- At least one accessibility-focused review session
- Voluntary anonymous feedback only

## Questions the pilot can answer

1. Did participants understand the relationship between system rules and individual pressure?
2. Did the experience increase knowledge of community-care options?
3. Did participants feel represented without being diagnosed or stereotyped?
4. What action did participants intend to take after the session?
5. Which accessibility barriers prevented full participation?

## Measures

| Measure | Evidence | Privacy rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | Session attendance and prototype completions | Aggregate counts only |
| Engagement | Completion and voluntary discussion participation | No individual behavioral profile |
| Learning | Two pre/post knowledge prompts | Anonymous; report only grouped results |
| Agency | Optional “one action I can take” reflection | Participant controls quotation consent |
| Accessibility | Barrier and accommodation feedback | Remove identifying health details |
| Capacity | Partners, trained facilitators, reusable materials | Verify contributions before credit |

## Claims we will not make

- The game treats or improves a mental-health condition.
- Participation prevents hospitalization, incarceration, homelessness, or substance use.
- An artist's participation validates a clinical or policy claim.
- Attendance alone proves social impact.

## Public evidence package

The repository should publish the intended outcomes before the pilot, record revisions, report limitations and negative findings, and tie every credited contribution to a verifiable action.
