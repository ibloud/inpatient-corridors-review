Different paths, continued goodwill

We explored a possible collaboration. It did not move forward, and Loptr Lab is continuing independently. We can still share work we appreciate: visit Webby’s World for Chris Webby’s own community.

This is part of the conversation behind 50 Ways to Leave Another: clarify boundaries, respect separate directions, and carry goodwill forward.

The adventures remain open to explore

Remembrance gives a project, character, or chapter a symbolic burial when carrying it forward has become too painful. In Loptr Lab’s creative framing, Caskets and Daddy’s Little Mortis connect to that act of letting a story rest. The personal experiences behind it remain private.

Continue with Violet’s Revenge or Violet’s Remembrance at the Made Sick adventure hub. Choose your own pace and how much you share.
