What the project is — and is not

Is

A participatory design prototype

Players encounter pressure, support, constraints, and choices about changing the system rather than simply managing individuals inside it.

Is not

Clinical care

It is not therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, crisis care, or clinical research. Participation is voluntary and prompts may be skipped.

Rights

Independent work

The continuing project uses independently created mechanics and documentation. Third-party names, music, likenesses, and protected material remain subject to their rights holders.

Safety and consent

No diagnosis or medical-record collection. Facilitators are not presented as therapists unless independently licensed and serving in that role. Crisis and peer-support resources are separated from the artistic experience.
