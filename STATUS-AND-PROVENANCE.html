Status and Provenance

Effective September 11, 2026

Current direction

Break the Grid is an active, independently continued Loptr Lab prototype built from mechanics developed within the Veiled Dominion design system.

Ren is not a participant in the continuing project. The repository does not state or imply a reason, assign blame, or invite another artist to mediate. Historical references remain only where needed to preserve an accurate development record.

Chris Webby has not approved, endorsed, or joined the project. He is offered a separate, optional opportunity to review the work privately and make his own decision. A September 12, 2026 discography lens documents Loptr Lab's evidence and interpretive design hypothesis; it is not attributed to Chris.

What continues

The continuing Loptr Lab work includes:

  • Board, pressure, tension, trauma, support, and phase-change mechanics
  • Cooperative system-redesign victory conditions
  • Consent-based care interactions
  • Accessible and low-demand participation concepts
  • Facilitator, evaluation, safety, and community-resource structures
  • Documentation of design decisions, authorship, and provenance

What does not continue without authorization

  • Artist names or person-based gameplay archetypes
  • Music, lyrics, recordings, artwork, footage, marks, likenesses, or established characters
  • Claims of endorsement, partnership, sponsorship, or approval
  • Public descriptions of private communications or interpersonal conflict
  • Award entries or credits for people who have not agreed to participate

Historical layer

The files ORIGINAL-VARIANT-CONCEPT.md and the repository history preserve earlier development language. Their presence documents provenance; it does not make those earlier relationships or proposed roles current.

Authorship and contribution rule

Loptr Lab claims only its original mechanics, writing, documentation, technical architecture, and other independently created material. Third-party rights remain with their respective rightsholders.

Any future contributor receives written scope, credit, ownership/licensing, compensation, correction, withdrawal, publicity, and exit terms before their contribution is commercially exploited or presented as a partnership.
