# Contributing

This review draft does not accept contributions that presume artist participation.

## Contribution loop

1. Claim a specific issue.
2. Make a reviewable change.
3. Document the source and reasoning.
4. Receive credit for the verified contribution.

Expressions of support, follows, shares, or private access do not create collaborator status.

## Required standards

- Preserve consent gates and non-affiliation language.
- Do not add lyrics, recordings, video stills, logos, likenesses, private messages, or medical information.
- Cite factual claims using traceable sources.
- Label speculation and unresolved rights questions.
- Do not diagnose artists, fans, contributors, or participants.
- Do not frame disagreement as proof of illness or moral failure.
- Do not collect sensitive participant data in repository issues.

## AI-assisted work

Disclose material AI assistance in the pull request. A human contributor remains responsible for accuracy, rights, safety, and editorial judgment.
